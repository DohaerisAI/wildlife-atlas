- New engine module `web/src/engine/living-earth/`: pack loader that refuses a bad manifest, CPU wind field, a surface shell (water + snow) and wind streaks. Story channels `water`, `snow`, `wind` switch them per chapter; wind is loudest over the Arabian Sea crossing (NE monsoon shows NE→SW).
- A caption on screen names the dataset for every material that is visible (the "Real" law). Quality tiers now set wind streak count and material extras (glints, ripples; off on base).
- **Data fix:** the v1 pack's water was wrong. Coarse JRC pyramid levels average only the pixels that ever held water, so one pond made a 40 km cell read ~80 %. `water_share()` multiplies recurrence by its fractional mask. `ee_probe.py` checks it against a 30 m reduction (Doyang 1.2 vs 0.56 true; Vembanad 11.8 vs 14.8; the old method gave 81 and 55). Pack rebuilt; water is now "expected % of the cell under water that month".
- JRC also counts near-shore sea as water, so the shader masks water and snow to land with the globe's land mask.
