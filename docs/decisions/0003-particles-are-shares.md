# 0003 · Particles are shares, not animals

**Status:** accepted · 2026-09-25

**Context:** For most species we only know where the species was recorded each month, not the path of any one animal.

**Decision:** Flow particles represent a share of a species' recorded presence, moved between monthly distributions by quantile coupling along a Hilbert curve. Tracked individuals (satellite tags) use a separate visual (a comet with a name) and are always labelled with their study.

**Consequences:** The UI must say so where particles appear. We never draw a single neat route for a whole species.
